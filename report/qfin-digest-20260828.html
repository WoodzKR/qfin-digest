Option-Implied Signals and Crash Risk: Predictability and Machine-Learning Evidence from U.S. Equity Options

옵션 시장 예측력이 시기마다 달라진다는 사실을 대규모 패널로 확인했다.

Re-tests six canonical option-implied signals across three market regimes, 2015-2026.

Baichuan Li, Mengxiao Wang

  • 코로나 이전에 세운 정설이 지금도 통하는지 다시 검증한다
  • 2015~2026년을 세 국면으로 쪼개 회귀와 부스팅 트리를 돌린다
  • 스머크는 힘을 잃고 IV 스프레드와 위험중립 왜도는 살아남는다
  • AI·초대형주 국면에서만 트리 모델이 선형 모델을 이긴다
방법
여섯 개 표준 옵션 신호를 회귀로 비교하고 XGBoost로 수익률과 급락을 예측한다.
데이터
미국 주식 옵션 10,026개 종목, 1,236만 기업-일 관측치를 2015~2026년 세 국면으로 나눠 검증한다.
시사점
스머크 대신 IV 스프레드와 위험중립 왜도를 알파 신호로 쓰고, 국면 판별 장치를 반드시 붙인다.
적용도 4/5
신호 정의가 명확해 바로 구현할 수 있지만, 미국 옵션 체인 이력 데이터를 유료로 사야 해 4점이다.
  • Canonical smirk results were established pre-COVID; do they still hold?
  • Splits 12.36M firm-days into three regimes, runs OLS plus boosted trees
  • Smirk decays to -0.006 (t=-1.5) and flips positive at 3 months
  • IV spread and risk-neutral skewness stay significant everywhere
Method
Univariate and joint regressions on six option signals, plus XGBoost on IV-surface, Greeks, activity and liquidity features.
Data
12.36M U.S. equity firm-day observations, 10,026 underlyings, 2015-2026, split into 2015-19, 2020-22 and 2023-26.
Use
Drop the smirk, keep Cremers-Weinbaum IV spread and risk-neutral skew, and gate any ML overlay on a regime detector.
Score 4/5
Signals are precisely defined and reproducible, but historical U.S. option chains are a paid vendor feed, so 4 not 5.

ORIGINAL ABSTRACTWe re-estimate canonical option-implied predictability evidence using a unified 2015--2026 panel of 12.36 million U.S. equity firm-day observations across 10,026 underlyings. We split the sample into three regimes: late-post-crisis low volatility (2015--2019), high-volatility transition (2020--2022), and AI/mega-cap concentration (2023--2026). The Xing et al. (2010) smirk--return relationship weakens steadily: the next-month univariate smirk coefficient falls from $-0.023$ $(t=-5.5)$ in 2015--2019 to an insignificant $-0.006$ $(t=-1.5)$ in 2023--2026, and turns positive at the three-month horizon $(+0.016,\ t=+2.1)$. In joint specifications with all six canonical signals, the smirk is insignificant throughout and again changes sign in the latest regime. By contrast, the Cremers--Weinbaum IV spread and a Bakshi et al. (2003)-style risk-neutral skewness measure remain significant across regimes and specifications. A boosted-tree benchmark using IV-surface, trading-activity, Greeks, and liquidity features outperforms linear models for next-month return prediction only in the AI/mega-cap regime, with $R^2_{\mathrm{OOS}}=+1.29%$ versus $+0.07%$. Permutation importance identifies different leading predictors by regime, and no canonical hand-engineered signal enters the top five. For firm-level five-day crash classification, AUC is highest in calm markets $(0.706,\ \text{XGBoost})$ and lowest during the high-volatility transition $(0.561)$. Overall, option-implied predictability is real but regime-dependent, and the post-2023 AI/mega-cap period differs sharply from the pre-COVID setting in which the canonical results were established.

From electricity prices to profits: multidimensional probabilistic forecasting for BESS trading

전력 가격의 다차원 확률예측으로 배터리 저장장치 매매 수익을 직접 예측한다.

Weron and Maciejowska forecast the joint distribution of hourly power prices and turn it into a distribution of daily BESS profits.

Tomasz Weron, Katarzyna Maciejowska

  • 일중 24시간 가격의 결합분포를 제대로 잡는 방법이 부족하다
  • Multiple Split(MS)에 추정창 길이별 예측 평균을 더한다
  • MS-ave가 PICP·CRPS·에너지 스코어 모두에서 앞선다
  • 운영비용이 0이 아닐 때 매매 전략 지원 효과가 특히 크다
방법
다중 분할(Multiple Split) 기법에 서로 다른 추정창 길이의 예측을 평균해 앙상블 가격분포를 만든다.
데이터
독일·스페인 하루전(day-ahead) 전력시장의 2021~2024년 가격 자료로 검증한다.
시사점
앙상블 시나리오를 그대로 넣어 일일 수익 분포를 뽑고, 충·방전 시각 선택과 리스크 한도 설정에 쓴다.
적용도 4/5
충·방전 규칙을 수익 분포에서 바로 도출하지만 배터리 사양과 비용 가정은 직접 정해야 하며, 유럽 하루전 가격은 ENTSO-E에서 무료로 받는다.
  • Point forecasts miss the 24-hour joint price structure
  • Multiple Split method plus averaging over estimation windows
  • MS-ave wins on PICP, CRPS and Energy Score
  • Edge is largest once operational costs are non-zero
Method
Multiple Split (MS) ensemble with forecast averaging across estimation windows of different lengths.
Data
German and Spanish day-ahead electricity markets, 2021-2024.
Use
Feed the price ensemble into a storage optimizer to pick charge and discharge hours and size the risk of each day's trade.
Score 4/5
Charge/discharge rules fall straight out of the profit distribution, but battery specs and cost assumptions are yours to set; European day-ahead prices are free from ENTSO-E.

ORIGINAL ABSTRACTThis article examines various methods of constructingmultidimensional probabilistic forecasts of electricity prices. Building on the Multiple Split (MS) method, it incorporates forecast averaging across estimation windows of different lengths and compares its performance with that of other, well-established methods. The research demonstrates that the ensemble representation of the price distribution is particularly useful in battery energy storage system (BESS) management. It enables the direct construction of probabilistic forecasts of daily profits. These forecasts can be used to determine optimal charging and discharging hours, as well as to support risk management decisions. The methods are evaluated using data from the German and Spanish day-ahead electricity markets from 2021-2024. The results indicate that theMS method with averaging (MS-ave) generally outperforms the other considered approaches in terms of Prediction Interval Coverage Probability (PICP), the Continuous Ranked Probability Score (CRPS) and the Energy Score (ES). Moreover, it is superior in supporting BESS trading strategies, particularly in case of non-zero operational costs.

Tabular Deep Learning for Algorithmic Trading: Cross-Regime Bayesian Optimisation for Equity Signal Generation

국면별 베이지안 최적화로 주식 신호를 뽑고 XGBoost·TabNet을 앙상블한다.

Tunes equity models for cross-regime trading performance, then ensembles XGBoost with TabNet.

Joshua Le Grice

  • 기존 평가는 하이퍼파라미터 단계에서 국면 강건성을 안 본다
  • 통계적으로 다른 3개 국면의 매매 성과를 목표로 튜닝한다
  • 하이브리드 앙상블이 연 51.26%, 샤프 2.44, 알파 0.423
  • 베타가 0에 가까워 초과수익은 종목 선택에서 나온다
방법
5개 모델을 국면별 매매 성과로 튜닝하고 XGBoost와 TabNet을 순위 합산으로 묶는다.
데이터
미국 대형주 약 300종목의 11년치 일간 데이터를 쓰고 테스트 구간 4개 분기로 검증한다.
시사점
단일 딥러닝 모델 대신 부스팅 트리와 순위 앙상블을 쓰고, 튜닝 목적함수를 국면별 성과로 바꿔 쓴다.
적용도 4/5
가격·재무 데이터는 구하기 쉽고 방법도 재현 가능하지만, 대체 데이터와 세부 진입·청산 규칙은 직접 설계해야 한다.

  • Bayesian optimisation targets returns across three regimes
  • Hybrid ensemble: 51.26% annual, 2.44 Sharpe, 0.423 alpha
  • Near-zero beta: gains come from selection, not exposure
Method
Five model classes tuned by Bayesian optimisation on regime-split trading metrics; XGBoost and TabNet merged by rank aggregation.
Data
Daily bars for ~300 US large-cap stocks over eleven years, tested across four out-of-sample quarters.
Use
Swap your tuning objective to regime-split trading performance, and rank-blend a boosted tree with a tabular net.
Score 4/5

ORIGINAL ABSTRACTAlgorithmic trading now represents a market exceeding $20 billion, where even marginal gains in signal robustness can translate into economically significant returns. Existing evaluations of equity prediction models do not explicitly target regime robustness during hyperparameter selection. Five model classes are trained on daily observations from approximately 300 large-cap US equities over eleven years, with Bayesian optimisation configured to target trading performance across three statistically different market regimes. Regime-robust hyperparameter selection is associated with out-of-sample generalisation, as signal precision remains above the random baseline across all four quarters of the test period, and portfolio performance slowly degrades under simulated input noise before collapsing beyond a defined threshold. No individual tabular deep learning architecture outperforms gradient-boosted trees, but combining XGBoost and TabNet using rank aggregation produces a Hybrid ensemble with an annualised return of 51.26%, a Sharpe ratio of 2.44, and a statistically significant CAPM alpha of 0.423 (p = 0.011). A near-zero beta indicates this outperformance is driven by stock selection, not market exposure. Alternative data plays a secondary role once technical and fundamental features are accounted for, as well as contributing more strongly on the short side than the long, and varies by model class. An interactive application makes these results explorable in real time, with live data integration the remaining step toward practical deployment.

ORIGINAL ABSTRACTWe document a large and robust pre-announcement drift in Japanese equity indices ahead of scheduled Bank of Japan (BOJ) monetary policy decisions: prices drift up steadily in the days before a meeting, peak immediately ahead of the decision, and do not continue afterward, earning an economically large and statistically significant excess return without elevated volatility. ORIGINAL ABSTRACTWe study statistical predictability in daily U.S. equity prices using only information available at the market open. Using SPY from February 1, 1993 through March 15, 2024, we benchmark XGBoost against Random Forest, LightGBM, Logistic Regression, and naive baselines under expanding-window walk-forward validation. After observing the current day's opening price and two lagged target-specific prices, the task is to predict whether the same day's close will be above or below the previous day's close. On the last 800 trading days, Logistic Regression attains the highest close-direction accuracy (71.09%), Random Forest reaches 61.20%, and XGBoost reaches 58.45% with a 95% bootstrap confidence interval of [54.94%, 62.08]. For XGBoost, close-direction accuracy rises to 72.7% when the predicted move exceeds 1%, but the usable sample falls to 154 observations. We also report Diebold-Mariano and McNemar tests, regime-specific results, SHAP feature importance, and an auxiliary 541-equity screen. The evidence supports a narrow statistical-finance conclusion: simple daily equity features contain detectable same-day directional information, but the result should be interpreted with careful sample-size accounting and limited economic claims.
